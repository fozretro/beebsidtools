Browse your High Voltage SID Collection in the Disc Creator, listen before you add, and let the Beeb player walk the disc for you.

## Player

- Press **A** on the menu and SIDPLAY plays each tune’s main song, then the next, then wraps
- In auto-play, each tune lasts its HVSC length when known, otherwise three minutes
- **Return** while a tune is playing skips to the next; **Escape** goes back to the menu
- Loading the next tune no longer freezes the player

## Disc Creator

- Open **HVSC** to browse a local collection: folders, search, listen, then Add
- Hear the original C64 SID (its default song) before it goes on the disc
- Search shows each tune’s HVSC length

## Notes

- Auto-play length needs HVSC song-length data in the collection; without it, tunes last three minutes
- If the Time column is blank on an older library, Re-index the folder
